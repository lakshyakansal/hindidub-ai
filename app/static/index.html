A REALISTIC NOTE 03 / 03

Built for control, not magic.

Voice similarity and timing depend on the model, language and source audio. Hindi can take more or fewer seconds than the original line; extreme time-stretching may sound unnatural. The video frames are copied, but the original soundtrack is replaced.
